About Our Team

We are a team who came together to explore the importance of plant health and the challenges caused by plant diseases. Through this project, we aim to understand common plant diseases, their symptoms, prevention methods and management techniques. Our goal is to spread awareness about protecting plants and promoting healthier growth through responsible plant care.

Our Mission

To spread accessible awareness of common plant diseases, encourage early symptom identification, and promote practical preventive practices that protect plant health in gardens, campuses, and agricultural communities.

Our Vision

We envision a future where widespread plant disease awareness and eco-conscious management contribute to thriving greenery, sustainable agriculture, and a healthier, greener environment for everyone.
